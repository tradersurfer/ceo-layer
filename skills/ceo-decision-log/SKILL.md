# CEO Decision Log

Use `ceo_decision_log` for every material decision.

- Record options, chosen option, and reason.
- Mark `needs_approval: true` for external, priced, shipped, legal, financial, or irreversible items.
- A logged decision is not approval. Approval comes from the owner, in the chat, for that item.
- Keep the log short, dated, and reviewable. Do not rewrite past entries; append a correction instead.
