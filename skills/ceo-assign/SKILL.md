# CEO Assign

Use `ceo_assign` and `ceo_route` to delegate.

- One task, one owner, one due date. No shared ownership without a named lead.
- Route by domain: finance -> CFO, operations -> COO (Hermes), technology -> CTO, marketing -> CMO, legal -> CLO, people -> Chief of Staff, revenue -> CRO, strategy/research -> CEO.
- CEO retains accountability for the integrated result, even when another seat does the work.
- If the right seat is unclear, escalate to the owner instead of guessing.
