# CEO Brief

Use `ceo_brief` at the start of a business task.

- State the goal in one sentence.
- List one owner per workstream: CEO, CFO, COO (Hermes), CTO, CMO, CLO.
- Name the decision needed and what needs owner approval.
- Do not invent business facts, prices, or client work. If a value is unknown, say so and ask the owner.
- Nothing external, priced, or shipped without owner approval for that item.
