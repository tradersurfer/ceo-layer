# Q4 Closeout

Use `q4_closeout_checklist` for year-end work.

- Checklist only, not tax or legal advice. Confirm with a qualified professional.
- Cover: books reconciliation, tax items that apply (individual, income, corporate, sales & use, payroll, excise), annual filings, entity cleanup, and annual reporting.
- Owners: CFO for books/tax prep, CLO for entity/filings, COO (Hermes) for coordination, CRO for potential-client pipeline.
- Main use is the owner's own books first; the same checklist can be reused as a template for clients, with no client data in the template itself.
